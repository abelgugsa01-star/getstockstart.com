GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Is this a one-time setup tool?

No. The first pass builds a clean baseline from your existing pricebook and invoices; after that you keep using it for every new invoice — weekly or monthly, whatever your purchasing rhythm is. That's why it's a subscription rather than a one-off.

How does it work with ServiceTitan?

Through your own export and import files — no API connection, nothing writes to your tenant automatically. You export your pricebook, StockStart patches the approved changes into that same workbook, and you import it back. StockStart is independent software and is not a certified or approved ServiceTitan integration.

Will it read every invoice perfectly?

No. Image quality and document layout can affect extraction. StockStart preserves the source evidence and surfaces uncertain values for review. We do not claim 100% OCR accuracy.

Are invoice quantities my current stock?

Not necessarily. Purchases, receipts, credits and stock snapshots have different meanings. StockStart helps review those relationships; a purchase history alone cannot establish current on-hand inventory.

What is Profitpin?

Profitpin LLC is the company behind StockStart, SaleLedger, LeafLedger and SubLimit. StockStart keeps a contractor's pricebook clean as new supplier invoices arrive.

IS THIS FOR YOU?

Built for one job. Here is whether it's yours.

StockStart keeps a ServiceTitan pricebook current from supplier invoices. That is the whole product.

A good fit if

  • Your shop runs on ServiceTitan and you can export and import the pricebook.
  • Supplier invoices arrive every week as PDFs, photos, emails or spreadsheets.
  • Someone in the office currently retypes parts and prices — or nobody does, and quotes drift.
  • You want uncertain lines held for a person, not written into the pricebook.

Not the right tool if

  • You are not on ServiceTitan (nothing here writes to other platforms yet).
  • You buy from one supplier with a clean price file you already import directly.
  • You need purchasing, PO approval or stock counts — this is pricebook upkeep, not inventory management.

What it is not

  • Not a ServiceTitan integration: no API, nothing writes to your tenant; you import the reviewed file.
  • Not an inventory system: invoice quantities are purchases, not stock on hand.
  • Not 100% OCR: creased photos get flagged for review with the source image.
