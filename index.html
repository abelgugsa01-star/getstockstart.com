GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
get started.

Can I use StockStart today?

StockStart is in development and is not open for customer pilots yet. Extraction, reconciliation and import acceptance work is still underway. This site introduces the product; it does not accept business documents.

Does it connect to ServiceTitan?

A direct ServiceTitan connection is not available. Materials workbook preparation is being developed, but preservation of a genuine tenant workbook and a controlled import still require validation. StockStart is not presented as a certified or approved ServiceTitan integration.

Will it read every invoice perfectly?

No. Image quality and document layout can affect extraction. StockStart preserves the source evidence and surfaces uncertain values for review. We do not claim 100% OCR accuracy.

Are invoice quantities my current stock?

Not necessarily. Purchases, receipts, credits and stock snapshots have different meanings. StockStart helps review those relationships; a purchase history alone cannot establish current on-hand inventory.

What is Profitpin?

Profitpin is the umbrella brand behind StockStart and future software products. StockStart focuses on helping contractors organize materials evidence into a reviewed starting library.
