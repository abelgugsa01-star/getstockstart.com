Vendor Invoice Matching for ServiceTitan Pricebooks

For HVAC, plumbing, and electrical shops: turn supplier invoice lines into proposed updates to your existing ServiceTitan pricebook. Review uncertain matches before importing, instead of treating every invoice description as a new material.

Try StockStart

A description is not a reliable item identifier

Two suppliers may describe the same part differently. A familiar description can also hide a different size or unit. Start with the vendor or manufacturer part number and compare units and the existing item record. StockStart flags uncertain matches so someone can review the source invoice.

Example: a box price versus an each price

An invoice lists a box of 20 fittings for $60. If the pricebook stores each fitting, the equivalent cost is $3 per fitting before any other applicable costs. Importing $60 as the each cost creates a 20-fold error. Confirm the pack size and unit from the source rather than assuming them. This example illustrates the review, not a promised automatic conversion.

Separate matched items from new-item proposals

Review existing-item matches, new-part proposals, and price differences as separate decisions. Keep unreadable values and ambiguous items in the review queue. Approving fewer well-supported changes is more useful than importing a larger workbook of guesses.

Export only what your team approves

StockStart works through your export and import files. Use your own ServiceTitan workbook as the baseline, review the proposed changes, and import the approved output through your normal process. It does not write automatically to your ServiceTitan account and is not a certified or approved ServiceTitan integration.

Questions before you start

Will every invoice scan read correctly?

No. Image quality and layout affect extraction. Check uncertain values against the source document.

Does StockStart replace the ServiceTitan pricebook?

No. It helps prepare reviewed updates to the pricebook you already use.

What should I keep after an import?

Keep the source invoice, original export, approved update file, and import result so you can trace an item change back to its evidence.
