Pricebook unit-cost checker.

Paste invoice lines and your current pricebook costs. This converts each pack price to a cost per unit, shows the change, and flags the classic mistake: a whole box cost entered as the cost of one. It runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded and there is no sign-up.

Columns: part or description, invoice price, units in that pack, pricebook cost per unit. Commas or tabs both work, so you can paste from a spreadsheet. The rows shown are made-up examples.
Pick what counts as unusual for your shop.
This checks arithmetic on what you paste. It does not read your invoices or connect to ServiceTitan, and a flag is a prompt to look, not a verdict. A pack quantity must match how your pricebook counts the item.

What the flags mean

  • Possible pack error: the new unit cost and the pricebook cost differ by about the pack quantity. Check the unit before changing anything.
  • Cost change: the new unit cost moved by more than your percent. Check it against the supplier's price change.
  • No pricebook cost: nothing to compare yet, so check that this isn't a duplicate of an existing item.
